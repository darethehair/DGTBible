About this experiment

DGTBible is an experimental original-language study Bible. The English DGT text is translated directly from Greek with AI assistance rather than revised from an existing English Bible. The embedded New Testament base text is Eberhard Nestle’s 1904 Greek New Testament, which is public domain. Difficult readings are intended to be checked against modern critical editions and manuscript evidence.
